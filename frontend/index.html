How Blockchain Eliminates Property Title Fraud

In traditional registries, double-selling occurs when corrupted databases or fake paper deeds allow one property to be sold simultaneously to two buyers. On Ethereum, the smart contract's modifier require(msg.sender == p.currentOwner) validates cryptographic ECDSA digital signatures at the consensus layer: once Rahul transfers PROP-101 to Priya, Priya's public key is the sole address capable of ever signing another transfer. Any second attempt by Rahul is rejected unconditionally by every node on the network.
